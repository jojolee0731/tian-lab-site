Ways to enquire

  • Graduate study: describe your intended degree, relevant preparation, and research interests.
  • Postdoctoral research: outline a scientific question and how your experience could contribute.
  • Research visits or student projects: explain your current affiliation, scope, and proposed dates.

These are enquiry routes, not a list of confirmed vacancies. Availability, eligibility, and admissions procedures must be confirmed for the relevant programme.
