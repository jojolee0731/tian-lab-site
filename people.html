Principal investigator

Xiaohe Tian, PhD

Professor / Principal Investigator / PhD Supervisor

Xiaohe Tian leads Tian Lab at West China Hospital, Sichuan University. His research connects brain barrier transport, multivalent interface design and molecular imaging to questions in neurodegenerative disease and nanomedicine. He trained at Anhui University and the University of Sheffield, followed by postdoctoral research in chemistry at University College London.
