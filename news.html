Research dispatch: Choroid plexus remodeling and CSF-mediated clearance in Alzheimer's disease.

Haolin Yin and Yuepeng Deng co-first-authored "Choroid plexus remodeling linked to impaired CSF-mediated clearance and Alzheimer's disease progression", published online in Alzheimer's & Dementia on 22 June 2026 (DOI: 10.1002/alz.71606). The study links choroid plexus remodeling to impaired CSF-mediated clearance and Alzheimer's disease progression, adding a barrier-and-clearance perspective to AD mechanisms.

2026.06

Research dispatch: Panyi Hu and Shuo Guo co-first-author an ACS Sensors study revealing Aβ species-dependent lipid-droplet remodeling in microglia.

Using the polarity-sensitive probe BODIPY-LD, the study maps lipid-droplet burden and polarity from cells to APP/PS1 brain tissue, shows that different Aβ species drive distinct microglial lipid-droplet states, and links LD accumulation to weakened phagocytosis. Panyi Hu and Shuo Guo are co-first authors; Xiaohe Tian and Jiahan Cheng are corresponding authors.

Research dispatch: Panyi Hu et al. report an LD-TTP AIEgen probe for reading the LD-LRP1-Aβ axis in NAFLD.

The paper, led by Panyi Hu with Dong Wang, Jiahan Cheng, and Xiaohe Tian as corresponding authors, links lipid-droplet remodeling to LRP1 spatial regulation and stage-dependent amyloid-β uptake in steatotic hepatocytes.
